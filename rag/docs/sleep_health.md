# Healthy Sleep: Guidance for Adults

## Recommended Amounts

Adults aged 18 to 64 should aim for seven to nine hours of sleep per night.
Adults over 65 need seven to eight hours. Consistency matters as much as
duration: going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, including
weekends, strengthens the body clock.

## Why Sleep Matters

During sleep the brain consolidates memory, clears metabolic waste, and
regulates the hormones controlling appetite, stress and growth. Regularly
sleeping less than seven hours is associated with impaired concentration and
mood, weakened immune response, weight gain, and elevated risk of
hypertension, type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease.

## Common Sleep Disorders

Insomnia, the difficulty falling or staying asleep despite adequate
opportunity, is the most common sleep complaint and is often driven by
stress, irregular schedules, caffeine, or screens before bed. Obstructive
sleep apnoea, in which breathing repeatedly stops during sleep, causes
unrefreshing sleep, loud snoring and daytime sleepiness, and raises blood
pressure and heart risk. Restless legs syndrome and shift-work disorder are
further common causes of poor sleep.

## Practical Sleep Hygiene

- Keep the bedroom dark, quiet and cool, around 18 degrees Celsius.
- Stop caffeine at least six hours before bedtime.
- Avoid large meals, alcohol and intense exercise close to bedtime.
- Put screens away one hour before bed; the light and engagement delay
  sleep onset.
- Get bright daylight in the morning, which anchors the circadian rhythm.
- Use the bed only for sleep; if awake longer than 20 minutes, get up and
  do something calm in dim light until sleepy.

## When to Seek Help

Consult a healthcare provider if snoring is loud with pauses in breathing,
if daytime sleepiness interferes with work or driving, if insomnia persists
beyond a few weeks, or if sleep problems began alongside low mood or
anxiety. Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is the
recommended first-line treatment for chronic insomnia and is more durable
than sleeping tablets.
